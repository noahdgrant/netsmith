# Compare with a git ref

What changed in the circuit since a branch, a tag or a commit: parts, nets, rails, the bill of materials and problems, with before and after, not lines of text (ADR 0059, the glossary's _Ref comparison_). The page is built on `GET /api/git`, `GET /api/compare?ref=` and `GET /api/git/file?ref=&path=`; progress is the socket's `progress` messages with `task: "compare"`.

## How to get to it

- The section **Compare** (key 8), or the palette: `Compare with a ref…`, `Show changes vs <ref>`, `Compare again`, `Stop comparing with a ref`, `Show changes in source`.
- A **status bar chip** `vs main: 10 circuit changes` once a ref is chosen (the lines of the source are in its tooltip) (its menu: show the comparison, show changes in the views, another ref, again, stop).
- The **switch** `Changes vs main` in the toolbar of the Schematic, the BOM, the Power tree and the System view, and a toggle in the Source's breadcrumbs: all one global switch (`showChanges`, kept in the browser).
- The file tab's right-click menu: `Show changes vs main`.

## How it looks

- **Header**: the ref picker (a popover with a search box: HEAD, the upstream and default branch, branches, tags, recent commits with hash, subject, author and age; the sentence about the work tree, `The working tree has uncommitted changes (compared as is).`), the board in scope (`All boards`, or one with its count), the switch and a refresh button.
- **The sentence** of the server (`1 part added, 1 removed, 2 values changed, …`), what it compared with (hash, subject, age, `working tree has uncommitted changes (compared as is)`, `cached` and the time), five **tiles** (Parts, Nets, Rails, BOM cost, Problems: `+` added, `−` removed, `~` changed, what shrank), and the **tabs**: Parts, Nets, Rails, BOM, Modules and interfaces, Problems, Source, each with a count.
- A **row**: a chevron (the fields that changed, or the pins that joined and left), the kind's icon, the path or name (`was …` when it moved or was renamed), the old and the new value as two chips (the old one struck, the new one tinted; the headroom of a rail that shrank is red), the designators of both sides, a badge (Added, Removed, Changed, Moved, Renamed, Renumbered, New, Resolved) and the links `Base` and `Head`. Renumbered parts are folded under the list (adding one capacitor renumbers the ones after it: not a change). Filter, and `Flat | By module | By change` (kept in the browser).
- **Click** a row: it is picked in every view (`selection.ts`; the chip in the top bar) and lit through the shared hover while the pointer is on it. `Head` opens the source at the line it is declared on; `Base` opens the comparison of that file; the schematic icon shows it in the Schematic. A removed thing has no head side, an added one no base side: that link is off.
- **The comparison of a file** (`DiffPanel`): the file at the ref and the file on disk (the editor's text when the file is open, so unsaved edits count), read only, side by side or inline (kept), changed lines coloured with the `--cmp-*` tokens, the words that changed marked inside the line, a hatched gap where a side has no line, `1 of 7 changes` with previous and next (F7 and Shift+F7), Escape closes. It opens over the Compare section's list (from a row's `Base`, or a file of the Source tab) or over the Source section's editor (`Show changes in source`, the file tab's menu, the status bar's `N changes`). The line diff is `compare/linediff.ts` (Myers, with the common start and end taken off first).
- **The marks** (`compare/compareDom.ts`, one subscriber, classes only: nothing is re-rendered and no drawing is replaced): the Schematic outlines changed parts amber, new ones green, renumbered ones neutral and dashed, lights the nets that changed softly, shows a legend chip and lists removed parts in the inspector under `Removed from this board` (or `module`) with the value they had and a `Base` link; the BOM has a `Change` column (`+1`, `−1`, the price), new lines marked and removed lines struck through at the end; the Power tree marks rails and converters and gives a rail whose headroom changed a badge `70 mA → 50 mA` (red when it shrank, green when it grew; a system's rails of every board); the System view marks boards; the Source has a bar in the margin (green new, amber changed, a red triangle where lines are gone) and `N changes` in the status bar.
- **States**: first analysis pending (skeleton), comparing (the server's stage text and the elapsed time, never a percentage; the stage dots), a ref that does not analyse (`<ref> cannot be compared`, the server's sentence and the ref's own problems, `Compare with another ref`), a name that is no ref, no change in the circuit (a check; with `Only the source differs` and the files when they do), not in a repository or git missing (an empty state with the reason), `N errors on disk` (the boards are as of their last good reading).

## Driving it

`scripts/editor-verify launch --git` makes the `two_boards` project a throwaway repository (`fixtures/gitrepo.mjs`: `main` and the tag `v1.0` are an earlier design with a debug header, no input capacitor, a 150 ohm resistor, the output net `VOUT` and 30 mA; `broken` has a typo in a type; `feature`, checked out, is the project as launched; an untracked `NOTES.md` makes the work tree dirty), then `scripts/editor-verify drive compare`. The files on disk are the plain fixture, so every other `two_boards` recipe runs unchanged in that session; `drive all` skips `compare` in a session launched without `--git`.

1. Key 8 opens the section: no chip yet, `Compare the circuit with a git ref`, an offer to compare with the default branch; the palette lists `Compare with a ref…`.
2. The picker lists HEAD, `main` (default branch), `broken`, `feature` (checked out), `v1.0` and two commits by author and age; the dirty sentence; a search for `v1` leaves the tag; Escape closes.
3. `main`: `1 part added, 1 removed, 2 values changed, 1 net renamed, 2 nets changed, 1 rail's headroom shrank from 70 mA to 50 mA, 2 rails changed`; five tiles; the chip `vs main: 10 circuit changes`; the board selector with each board's count.
4. Parts: `stage.input_cap` added, `debug` removed, `r1` `150 → 100`, the LED changed, two renumbered parts folded; expand `r1` for its fields; filter, group by module. Nets: `3V3 was VOUT`, `VIN` gained a pin. Rails: the headroom `70 mA → 50 mA`, the new value red. BOM: lines added and removed. Modules and Problems: empty states with a check. Source: `src/main.nsm` modified, `NOTES.md` untracked, with totals.
5. Click `r1`: the selection chip; hover lights; `Head` and `Base` (disabled where a side has no line); the comparison of the file (`150ohm` on the left, `.r1: Resistor {}` on the right, coloured lines, marked words, hatched gaps, `1 of 7 changes`, next, inline with two columns of numbers, read only, Escape); the same view over the Source's editor; the file tab's menu.
6. The switch: the Schematic of `TwoBoards/led` (D1 and R1 outlined as changed in the comparison's colours, the socket as renumbered, a legend, the removed header in the inspector, the drawing not replaced: a mark on the `svg` survives and no node is added or removed), the BOM (a `Change` column, `+1` on two new lines, three removed lines struck through at the end), the Power tree (the badge `70 mA → 50 mA`, changed rails of both boards), the System view (both boards), the Source (bars and `7 changes`); `Hide changes` in the palette leaves no class of a mark anywhere; the chip's menu turns them on again.
7. A reload keeps the ref (the comparison is there with no choice made). An error written to `src/main.nsm` is a new problem (`1 error on disk`, the Problems tile `1 new`), gone when the file is right.
8. `HEAD`: `No changes in the circuit`, `Only the source differs`, `NOTES.md`. `broken` while the answer is held back: the stage text and the time, no `%`, the chip `comparing…`; then the sentence of the ref that does not analyse with `unknown type`, the chip `cannot compare` and the way out. A name that is no ref says so (the browser logs the server's 404: the recipe lets that one error through with `allowConsole`).
9. Not a repository: a second `netsmith editor` over a copy of the project without `.git` answers `GET /api/git`; the page is given that answer and shows the state with the reason and no picker.

## Beside the rest (`compare-interactions`)

The marks, the hover and the pick all write to the same elements, and the views draw those elements again; what the page does about it:

- **One element, three signals.** A part of the schematic can be outlined as changed (`cmp-changed`), lit by the shared hover (`hover-lit`) and picked (`mark`). The stylesheet decides once, in `compare.css`: the pick owns the outline (the mark's rules are `:not(.mark)`), the hover adds its light to the glow after the mark's (`filter` is one property, so the combined rule lists both), a card of the power tree or the system keeps the mark's ring, adds the hover's outside it, and a picked card has the pick's ring with the mark's outside that. The same for the board's parts. `--cmp-c` is the mark's colour on the element, so each rule is written once.
- **A view that is drawn again.** A card whose `class` changes (picked, dimmed) loses the classes the subscribers put on it. Both subscribers (`hoverDom.ts`, `compare/compareDom.ts`) now watch the `class` attribute of the page too and put back what is missing; they change only the difference (`domMarks.ts: reconcile`), so a write that changes nothing is not made and they do not answer themselves, and neither takes the other's classes.
- **Escape.** The window's key runs before a panel's own. The comparison of a file, the ref picker and every menu of the status bar carry `data-esc-closes`, which holds back `Back` (a click-through's way back) and `Clear the selection`; Escape closes the panel and nothing else.
- **The status bar** keeps, as the window narrows, the problems, the chip, the unsaved files, the cursor and the connection's dot; the analysis time, the language and the theme (the gear has it) go first, then the chip's `vs main:`, the connection's words, the board's name and the source options (`responsive.css`, a container of the bar, so a larger size folds earlier). The chip's menu opens from the chip's left edge. A job's line ends in an ellipsis.
- **Size and density.** The section's rows, tabs, chips and the diff view's bar are tokens (`--row-lg`, `--tab`, `--bar`, `--h-xs`), so compact tightens them and 125 % grows them. The diff view's text is the source editor's own size (`--source-fs`), as the Source's: the page's size does not change it.
- **Boards with planes.** The marks add only `cmp-` classes (and `data-cmp-delta`), and no rule of `compare.css` names `data-layer`, `data-plane` or `.plane-label`; `model.test.ts` holds that. (A net's mark on a plane's rectangle is a class nothing draws.)
- **Not a repository.** The palette's `Compare with a ref…` opens the section, which says why, and leaves no picker waiting; `Show changes` says so in a toast. The splash is the same as in any project.

## Gotchas

- The page reads `/api/compare` for every board. **One model of the counts** (round 6): the section's scope is `All boards` (the one board of a project that has one) whatever board is in front, so the sentence, the tiles, the tab counts, the rows, the `N circuit changes` under the sentence (`data-testid="cmp-total"`: parts, nets, rails, modules and interfaces) and the status chip are one number; a board chosen in the selector filters all of them down, and the sentence is that board's own (`CompareBoard.summary`). The chip stays the whole project's. The Source item of the status bar says `N lines changed in this file` (the lines of the file in the editor against the file at the ref: a changed line is one, not two).
- Round 6, in the section: the BOM tab is grouped Added, Removed, Changed with counts; a BOM cost that is not a number (`unknown`) is a quiet tile with an (i) saying `No prices in the catalogs yet`; how long the comparison took (`cached, 0.0 s`) is in the tooltip of its age; the page is at most `--page-w` (80 rem) wide and centred; the diff view's scroll to a change ends on a whole line (`snapToLine`: a line cut by the header's edge is scrolled out, or in when the file ends there).
- The marks are overlays, never a recolouring: the schematic's rectangle outside the pick's halo (`.part-cmp`), the board's `rect.cmp-rect` (2 px outline and an 8 % tint over the courtyard, drawn by `pcb/Canvas.tsx` from the store's marks; the group's `cmp-*` class stays for the subscribers and hover), the source's gutter bar of 5 px with a tint across the line and ticks in an overview ruler at the right edge (`.cm-chg-ruler`). The PCB toolbar has the switch (`.pcb-bar [data-testid="changes-switch"]`).
- `dirty` is any change in the work tree, untracked files included: `compare HEAD` is the comparison of the work tree. A file that does not build leaves its boards as of the last good reading (`stale`), and the sentence starts with `N errors on disk:`.
- The marks need the head's drawing: a removed part has none, so it is listed (inspector, BOM) and not drawn. A net or a rail is found by its name on the head side; a rail card of a system is told apart by the board it says it is on (`data-cmp-board`).
- The gutter keeps a hidden copy of its widest mark to size itself: a recipe counting marks counts those with a height.
- The base side's lines are the ref's own, as the server says them (`baseAt`); they are not shifted to where the line is now.
- Wishes for the server: `Compare.problems` and each board's `problems.new` say which board a problem is on only by `board`; a `kind` on a changed part (it has none, so a changed row cannot say `Capacitor`); the head side's `designator` for a net's pins that left (it is the ref's).
