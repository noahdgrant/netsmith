# netsmith

A language and toolchain for describing circuits as text and turning them into files for CAD tools.

## The terms

A term is in the file of its first letter, in `docs/glossary/`, and the entries of a file are in alphabetical order. A new term goes where it sorts.

Each entry says what the word means here, and most end with an `_Avoid_:` line, the words to use no other way for that thing. To see every word to avoid: `grep -h '^_Avoid_' docs/glossary/*.md`.
